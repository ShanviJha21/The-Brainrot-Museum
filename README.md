# The-Brainrot-Museum
The Brainrot Museum is a digital museum celebrating the chaotic, hilarious and ever-evolving world of internet culture. Featuring nine curated categories spanning memes, music, books, history and more, it blends a sophisticated black-and-gold museum aesthetic with interactive exhibits, playful exploration and a Brainrot Identity Quiz. Visitors can discover their internet personality and earn a personalised Certificate of Brainrot.
